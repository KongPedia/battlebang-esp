# Plugin and product licensing

This project selects identities from `konglabs-agent-skills` through its [host configuration](.agents/plugin-setup.md).
Reusable plugin payloads and their licenses/notices remain with the provider and host cache; they are not vendored here.
Review actual payload provenance/terms before use or redistribution. Activation settings do not attest installed bytes.

The template does not choose this product's license. Preserve applicable attribution when redistributing material.
